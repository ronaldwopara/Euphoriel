# 2026-10-24 Call — ecom double down

Who:: Renald + client (brand side)
Channel:: call. Transcript was auto-speech and messy. This note is the cleaned sense, not a word-for-word record.
Project:: [[Euphoriel]], [[Stretch migration]], [[Homepage rebuild]]
Related:: [[2026-10-24 Stretch theme migration]], [[Saturday Stretch meeting]], [[Brand and audience]]

## What they said

They stopped trade shows / physical retail so they can put the same effort into e-commerce. Physical is 1:1 and capped by headcount. Ecom plus ads is the salesperson that runs 24/7. Do not do anything crazy. Fix the site, add tracking, then ads.

The homepage was stood up so people at trade shows could find them and buy. No strategy. Never revisited. Attention is back on the site now.

Order of work they stated:

1. Fix the homepage (clarity, trust, conversion).
2. Later, a dedicated ads landing page. Do not send paid traffic to the homepage. Too many options.
3. Aim to have the major fixes + a landing page + ads in time to learn from **Black Friday (November)**.

Search Console: blogs get a lot of impressions (one page ~20k). People read and do not buy oils. Two reads on the call: product choice is unclear, or vegan / plant-based is not said out loud. Queries like face-oil mixing ratio and ingredients show up. SEO consultant last year drove traffic, not conversion. They called it not an SEO problem. Idea: put those queries on product pages, and write winter-season blogs. Blog lives in the footer. They decided to leave it there.

Thinking bigger than a $150 day: 20k–100k months.

On the theme: **discontinue Replo**. Current site is a Replo (and maybe Palo Alto) mix. They want something fresh for the homepage, not a clone of what is live, and not a long custom build. Drop in an agreed template and reuse assets. Conversion is the goal.

What they said is broken on the current homepage: footer, reviews (Judge.me / review app not connected; names look staged), shop block, no trust, parts feel disconnected. A stranger would not buy. Compared the shop page to Ordinary and others.

References they looked at: The Ordinary, Dermalogica, Skin Nerd, CeraVe, 111Skin (too luxury / sharp / expensive), one One/One Skin or 111Skin send on WhatsApp. Ordinary-style sites: hero video, bestsellers, short brand story, skin-concern entry, reviews, UGC, real people, lots of content packed cleanly. They already have UGC and a February photoshoot. Reuse, up-res if needed.

New brand / new product line is coming (more sophisticated, mid next year). Use now through December on the current Euphoriel site as the test engine. When the rebrand is ready, swap images, names, banner; do not start from zero. Soft-launch the new brand on the already-optimized site. Eventual banner / redirect like Twitter → X.

Direction: **affordable luxury**, closer to Ordinary than 111Skin. Travel sizes so someone can try (~$6–7 talk on the call) vs full size. Audience they landed on: **working class, about 30–45**, quality / anti-aging, not students. Real humans on the site. Content-heavy for skincare (confidence), still clean.

Product page idea: open on the matched oil (example: Harmonic), 4–5 key ingredients, how it pairs, then "unlock 15% by bundling" so the bundle is introduced without dumping the whole builder first.

Also floated: body cream / body wash line; men's skincare as a possible later brand (caution: do not attack other brands by name). Face oil images not zoomed in enough.

Close of call: meet **Saturday** to discuss homepage and campaigns. Until then, focus there.
