# Brand and audience

From [[2026-10-24 Call — ecom double down]]. Soft, not a locked brand book.

## Who it is for now

- About **30–45**, working class
- Quality, anti-aging, face oils and serums
- Not the student / "still in school" buyer
- Canada-first brand (said they should read as a Canadian brand)

## How it should feel

- Affordable luxury
- Closer to **The Ordinary** (accessible, content, clean) than **111Skin** (sharp, high-end, $500+ territory)
- Real people, UGC, video, packed-but-clean pages
- Travel size + full size so try-before-commit is possible

## What is coming

- New product line / new brand, more sophisticated, target mid next year
- This year: make Euphoriel the conversion engine and testbed
- Soft launch: put new products on the already-working site, then banner / redirect when the name changes
- Male and female models stay (that was the original launch idea too)
- Possible later: men’s line. Do not attack other brands by name.
