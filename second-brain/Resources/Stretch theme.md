# Stretch theme

Store listing: [Stretch / Stretch preset](https://themes.shopify.com/themes/stretch/presets/stretch)
Maker:: Maestrooo
Price:: $400 USD
Version:: 2.1.1 (26 Aug 2026)
Presets:: Stretch, Snow, Diffuse
Reviews:: 88% positive (43)

Pitch: expansive layouts, dynamic grid, 30+ sections, high-end look without a full custom theme. Built for storytelling brands; lists a lot of conversion chrome.

## Conversion features they paid for

Promo banners, tiles, popups. Countdown. Trust badges. Stock counter. Quick view. Cross-sell (including cart drawer). Recently viewed / recommended. Product badges. In-menu promos. Press coverage. FAQ. Free shipping bar (Markets-aware). Slide-out + sticky cart. Quick buy.

## Merchandising that fits Euphoriel

Ingredients / usage info. Product tabs. Image zoom, rollover, hotspots, galleries. Lookbooks. Before/after slider. Color swatches. Size chart. Product video.

## What it will not do for us

- Skin quiz scoring + Klaviyo profile/event write
- Build-your-bundle with `?face=` / `?add=`
- The welcome email itself (that stays in Klaviyo)

Those stay custom on top of Stretch. [[Klaviyo and quiz stack]]
