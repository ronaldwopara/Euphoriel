## Homepage outline to lock (say it out loud)

1. Hero: one line + one CTA. Video if a usable file exists today, else the best still. CTA = quiz or shop bestsellers, pick one.
2. Bestsellers / four oils, zoomed in.
3. Brand in three sentences. Canadian, vegan, waterless. Audience 30–45.
4. Skin concerns → [[Skin quiz]]
5. Real reviews (connected app, real names)
6. UGC / real people
7. Free shipping over $75 + 30-day guarantee
8. Footer that works

CTA rule for today: **one primary action above the fold.** Quiz is the right one if they agree people do not know which oil. Shop is the right one if they want a faster buy. Do not leave with both fighting.

## Five decisions you must write before you hang up

1. Theme: Stretch, Stretch preset, unpublished copy. Yes / no.
2. Above-the-fold CTA: Quiz or Shop.
3. Hero: named video file, or named still.
4. Black Friday offer in one line (example: 20% sitewide / extra 5% on 3+ bundle / gift with two oils). One URL: homepage or a new landing page.
5. Reviews: app name + who logs in today.

If any of those is still "we'll see," the meeting failed.

## What you are not deciding today

New brand name, men’s line, body wash, SEO program, 20k–100k month plan. Write them under Later if they come up.
